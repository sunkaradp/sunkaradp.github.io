Project risks rarely appear first in formal documentation. They often surface in everyday project communication: email threads, changing requirements, delayed decisions and unresolved dependencies.

In this article, I look at how AI agents can help turn unstructured project emails into structured risk intelligence for German startups. The workflow focuses on extracting evidence, matching it to project risks, classifying the result and keeping the output traceable for people who need to act on it.

This is an applied exploration of using AI-assisted analysis to reduce repetitive project coordination work. It is not a claim that an autonomous agent can replace project managers or make decisions without human review.
