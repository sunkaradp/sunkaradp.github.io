Master's thesis · Work in progress

From organisational email to project knowledge.

A research study on identifying project-related conversations in large email collections and examining how language models can help organise the information they contain.

ResearcherDurgaprasad Sunkara
ProgrammeMSc Applied Data Science & Analytics
ContextSRH Hamburg · AIcoo

Research question

How can messages associated with a particular project be identified when project language changes over time, and how can the resulting information be represented in a useful project knowledge resource?

Approach

The work studies a staged retrieval and evaluation process, combining information-retrieval techniques with language-model assistance. The emphasis is on assessing the quality and usefulness of the resulting project-level information.

Methods, experiments and evaluation are still being refined as part of the thesis.

Current status

Research and implementation are in progress. Findings will be shared only after they have been checked, contextualised and approved for public release.

Research privacy
This public page intentionally does not publish source messages, mailbox details, project-specific correspondence, raw data, interim results, prompts or internal evaluation material.
