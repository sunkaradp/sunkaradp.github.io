# About Me

Hi, I'm **Durgaprasad Sunkara**.

<center><img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/profile.jpg" width="300" alt="Durgaprasad Sunkara"></center>

I'm a DevOps and Platform Engineer based in Hamburg, Germany. I work across Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, automation and observability. I enjoy building systems that are reliable to operate and straightforward to understand when something goes wrong.

Most recently, at **AIcoo GmbH**, I built and operated the DevOps environment for an LLM-based product. The application included a FastAPI backend, frontend and PostgreSQL, deployed on k3s at Hetzner Cloud. I created 28 Kubernetes manifests, organised development and staging overlays with Kustomize, and built the delivery workflow using GitHub Actions, a self-hosted runner and GHCR. My work also covered secrets management, monitoring and recovery.

Earlier in my career, I worked across **Skillmine** and **Test Yantra**, building more than three years of experience across DevOps, cloud and infrastructure engineering.

I'm completing an **MSc in Applied Data Science and Analytics at SRH University, Hamburg**, expected March 2027. My master's thesis explores identifying project-related information in email collections and evaluating automated project knowledge extraction.

## What I work with

- **Cloud and infrastructure:** AWS, Hetzner Cloud, Terraform, Ansible
- **CI/CD and GitOps:** GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Argo CD, GHCR
- **Observability:** Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Alertmanager
- **Development and data:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Linux
- **Operations and security:** RBAC, secrets management, Trivy, recovery procedures

## Current focus

I'm interested in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud and SRE roles where I can work on infrastructure, deployment systems and production reliability. I'm based in Hamburg and open to opportunities across Germany and Europe.
