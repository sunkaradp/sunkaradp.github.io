My master's thesis investigates how information scattered across large collections of organisational conversations can be brought together into a coherent project-level view.

The work combines information retrieval and language-model-assisted analysis. Research details, source material, interim findings and evaluation artefacts are intentionally not published on this portfolio page while the thesis is in progress.
