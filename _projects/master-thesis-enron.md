My master's thesis explores how project-related emails can be identified in a large email corpus and how well an LLM-generated project wiki represents the underlying project information.

The repository includes data loading, keyword retrieval, graph clustering, LLM-assisted decisions for borderline emails, and evaluation scripts. The dataset itself is not committed because of its size.
