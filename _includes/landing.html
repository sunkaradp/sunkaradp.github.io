A little about me

Engineering with an operational mindset.

Building systems that are understandable, repeatable and reliable.

I’m a DevOps and Platform Engineer with 3+ years of experience across cloud and infrastructure engineering. At AIcoo GmbH in Hamburg, I built the DevOps environment for an LLM-based product with a FastAPI backend, frontend and PostgreSQL, running on k3s at Hetzner Cloud.

My work included Kubernetes configuration with Kustomize, development and staging environments, GitHub Actions CI/CD with a self-hosted runner, GHCR image delivery, secrets management, monitoring and recovery planning.

Previously, I worked at Skillmine, Test Yantra and Tech Mahindra. I’m completing an MSc in Applied Data Science and Analytics at SRH University Hamburg, with my thesis research underway.
